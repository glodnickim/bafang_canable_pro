Ride core tuning

These are not bank or level settings. One set of values applies to both banks and all five assist levels. Changing the bank or level selector above has no effect on anything in this section, and it has its own Read/Write buttons.

Startup boost decay

How fast the per-level "Startup boost" fades as cadence rises. The strength and end cadence are per level (above); this decay shape is shared.

Start condition and ride latch

"Crank movement to start" is the second half of the start condition — the first half is the per-level "Minimum pedal load" above, and both must be met. The latch then keeps assist alive after a legal start, so very light pedalling and the crank dead-spots don't cut power. Braking, backpedalling, a fault, or stopping the cranks still cut instantly.

RUN torque smoothing — anti-pulse "left leg / right leg"

A slower estimate of pedal effort used to compute power/eMTB while riding, so the motor follows your average effort instead of each individual leg push. Start, boost and the safety cut stay on the fast signal. The window is measured in degrees of crank rotation, not milliseconds — so one setting behaves the same grinding up a climb at 50 rpm and spinning at 110 rpm, which a fixed time could never do. 180° = half a turn = one leg. 0° = off (raw signal). Higher = smoother but slower to react to a change in effort.

Load calibration (optional)

Optional — the bike always rides fine on the default scale (740 mV zero, 1620 mV = 60 kg) even if you never do this. Calibrating only makes the "kg" readings and level thresholds match your actual pedal/sensor more precisely.

  1. Zero point: take all load off the pedal (crank position doesn't matter), then press "1. Start (no load)". This captures the sensor's resting signal as zero.
  2. Full scale: enter the weight you're about to hang in Reference weight (kg) (5-30 kg), hang exactly that known weight on the pedal with the crank horizontal, then press "2. Capture load". This measures the signal at that load and computes the full-scale point.
  3. Save: remove the weight, make sure the bike is at a complete standstill, then press "3. Save" to commit the new zero/full-scale pair to the controller. Saving while moving is rejected.
  4. Cancel aborts a Start/Capture in progress without changing anything already saved. Restore default discards any saved user calibration and goes back to the factory estimated scale — use it if a calibration attempt went wrong.

Full charge voltage — 100% anchor (0x602B)

Pack voltage treated as 100% state of charge. Measure it on the battery right after a full charge and balancing (e.g. ~45.9 V for an 11S pack). On the next power-up, if the pack sits at or above this voltage, SOC is anchored to 100% — works even if the charger bypasses the controller shunt. Saves at a full standstill; kept across restarts. Needs eVistDrive firmware with 0x6028 v2.
